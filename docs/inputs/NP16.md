## Scope

Lean proves the corrected sufficient OU-interpolation argument needed to establish the finite-dimensional convex-set Ehrhard inequality.

The full Jensen equivalences, necessity arguments, general measurable-function variants and remaining illustrations are not formalized here. The proved sufficient direction has the corrections and regularity restrictions stated above.
