## Scope

Lean proves Tsai’s dense-time uniqueness mechanism on compact time intervals for processes satisfying the encoded moment, flow, and independence hypotheses through order four, together with the four-particle estimate needed to close it.

The mollified-SHE construction and almost-sure path continuity are not formalized here. The KPZ polymer limit is not identified with that mollified-SHE construction.
