## Scope

The convex-set Ehrhard consequence is proved by NP16 interpolation, while the stochastic-game theorem and its further applications are not formalized.

The stochastic-game representation, controls and strategies, reverse Brascamp–Lieb theorem and generalized-mean results are not formalized here.
