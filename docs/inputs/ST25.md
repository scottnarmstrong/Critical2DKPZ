## Scope

Lean proves the corrected limiting weighted kernel bounds on compact time intervals and collision-chain summability needed by the Tsai uniqueness argument, including the relevant four-particle application.

The regularized ε > 0 construction and convergence theory are not formalized here. The proof uses corrected collision-chain summability rather than the printed universal-constant formula.
