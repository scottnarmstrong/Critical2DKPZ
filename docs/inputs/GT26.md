## Scope

A mean-centred fixed-parameter delayed heat-start version of the local log-log CLT is proved, together with finite lattice block expansions. The moving-parameter theorem, source centring and conditional-GMC proof are outside this scope.

The completed heat-start CLT is [`Critical2DKPZ.shf_logNormality`](../../Critical2DKPZStatements/ShfLogNormality.lean).
