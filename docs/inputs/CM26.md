## Scope

Finite-slab annealing, limiting conditional bounds, approximate composition and dense-time flow are proved for the Gaussian period-two polymer, as is the Euler-shifted Gamma-integral dictionary. The source’s mollified-SHE conditional-mean proposition, continuum path-space construction and Volterra-derivative identity are not asserted verbatim.

The moment and flow inputs are supplied in [the Gaussian polymer construction](../../Critical2DKPZ/Assembly/Final.lean).
