## Scope

Lean proves the adapted Ehrhard representation, weak closure, finite-domain log-MGF convexity, normalized negative-moment comparisons and the heat-profile log-SHF Ehrhard property. The inverse-moment lower bound is supplied at p = −1. The full GMC results, all-order comparisons and extended-value theorem without moment hypotheses are outside this scope.

The model inputs to the inverse-moment bound are supplied in [the non-Gaussianity proof](../../Critical2DKPZ/Provider/CriticalKPZNonGaussian.lean).
