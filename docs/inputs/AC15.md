## Scope

The AC15 convex specialization needed historically by KPZ follows from the stronger all-real Gaussian-convexity theorem proved by the independent FMP25/NP16 argument.

The general three-function inequality, Boué–Dupuis representation, Parisi PDE results and unique-minimizer theorem are not formalized here.
