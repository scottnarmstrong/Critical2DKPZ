Errata are errors in the pinned sources; deviations are deliberate differences in scope, conventions or proof methods. A supplied argument for a true but compressed step is a deviation. Counts are substantive entries, not occurrences; a grouped entry counts once. Cross-reference aliases, unverified intermediate correspondences and optional proofreading notes are excluded.

## Proof methods and limits of coverage

The KPZ proof constructs SHF laws by polymer moments and dense-time uniqueness, proves Gaussian convexity through Ehrhard’s inequality, and proves delayed heat-start fluctuations by lattice blocks. Non-Gaussianity uses concentration and differentiation. These changes supply the conclusions stated by the exported Lean theorems; they do not formalize every theorem in the cited input papers. Conditional helper theorems prove implications with their displayed assumptions. Their existence alone does not prove a source theorem with those assumptions removed.

The scope is the specified Gaussian planar polymer, parity and endpoint conventions, test classes and parameter windows. General-disorder universality, arbitrary renewal kernels, full all-particle operator semigroups, source path-space constructions, conditional Gaussian multiplicative chaos, and sharp discrete log-concavity constants are not inferred from the specialized results here. See the [per-paper reports](inputs/README.md).

## Sources used only for comparison or replaced arguments

AC15, Chen26 and Ivan16 provide alternative proof methods; the formalization does not reproduce their stochastic-control or boundary-value theories. BCT25, BTZ26 and CSZ20 supply model comparisons rather than theorems used as additional axioms. Citations to unavailable originals, including BGT89, Hoggar’s finite convolution theorem, Bertoin’s crossing identity, Folland, Rudin and Kallenberg, are replaced by the specific directly proved results identified in the input reports. An unavailable original is not evidence that its theorem is false.
