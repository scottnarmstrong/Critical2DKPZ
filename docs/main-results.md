# The 22 results proved in this formalization

This page lists the 22 Lean statements proved in this repository for *The Critical 2D KPZ Process* (Berger,
Caravenna, Clark, Dunlap, Gu, Liu, Nakajima, Sun, Tsai, Xie and Zygouras): the main conclusions about the critical SHF
and the critical KPZ process (items 1 to 10), followed by selected supporting results from the paper and from the
literature (items 11 to 22). Each link opens the file with the exact Lean statement; the definitions it uses are in
[`Critical2DKPZ/Definitions`](../Critical2DKPZ/Definitions/). Theorem and page numbers refer to
[arXiv:2610.31614v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31614v1).

## Conventions

The lattice model uses independent standard Gaussian disorder and planar nearest-neighbour simple random walk, with disorder at the terminal time included and at the initial time excluded. A continuum point $x$ is represented by the fixed-tie nearest point $[\sqrt{N/2}\,x]$ of $\lbrace z\in\mathbb Z^2:\ z_1+z_2\in2\mathbb Z\rbrace$. Nonnegative clocks are rounded by $[r]=2\lceil r/2\rceil$. For sufficiently large $N$, the critical tuning is exactly

$$
\sigma_N^2=R_N^{-1}(1+\vartheta/\log N),
\qquad \beta_N=\sqrt{\log(1+\sigma_N^2)}.
$$

Here $R_N=\sum_{n=1}^{N}P(S_{2n}=0)$ is the expected number of returns to the origin of the simple random walk $S$ up to time $2N$.

Uniformity over compact parameter intervals is stated where proved; no general-disorder or general-walk universality assertion is implicit.

**The critical SHF.** For each fixed interval $0\le s\lt t$, a critical stochastic heat flow (SHF) is a measurable random measure whose pair measure and first marginal are almost surely locally finite; its compact-test pairings are the joint limits of the polymer pairings ([`IsCriticalSHF`](../Critical2DKPZ/Definitions/IsCriticalSHF.lean)). The definition does not require almost-sure continuous paths. Its existence is item 1.

**The associated KPZ process.** We call a family $H$ associated with $Z$ when, for $t>0$, bounded continuous $h_0$ and test functions $\varphi\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R^2)$, the regularized pairings converge in probability to $H_t^\vartheta(h_0,\varphi)$ as $\delta\downarrow0$ ([`IsCriticalKPZ`](../Critical2DKPZ/Definitions/IsCriticalKPZ.lean)); $H$ is determined only up to null sets. Write $H_t^\vartheta(\varphi)$ when $h_0=0$. Statements 3 to 9 and 22 hold for every critical SHF $Z$ with parameter $\vartheta$ and every family $H$ associated with $Z$. Null sets and measurability qualifications are part of the statements; the comparison inequality holds almost surely.

**Notation.** $Z_{s,t}(f,g)=\int f(x)g(y)\,Z_{s,t}(dx,dy)$, $g_u(z)=(2\pi u)^{-1}e^{-\lvert z\rvert^2/(2u)}$, $g_\delta^{(x)}=g_\delta(\cdot-x)$, and $q_n(z)$ is the probability that the simple random walk is at $z$ after $n$ steps.

## The results

1. **[SHF existence](../Critical2DKPZStatements/ExistsIsCriticalSHF.lean)** — For every $\vartheta\in\mathbb R$ there is a probability space carrying a critical two-dimensional SHF in the polymer-approximation sense just specified. (Theorem A, p. 5.)

2. **[Construction and convergence of the critical KPZ process](../Critical2DKPZStatements/CriticalKPZMain.lean)** — Every such SHF admits an associated family $H$. For each fixed $t>0$ and bounded continuous $h_0$, $H$ is almost surely in every $H^{-a}_{\mathrm{loc}}$, $a>0$, and its test evaluations are measurable with respect to the SHF. For each $0\lt \delta \lt t$ the regularized field is almost surely locally integrable. As $\delta \downarrow 0$ it converges in probability in each localized $H^{-a}$ seminorm, and its test pairings converge in every finite $L^p$, $p\ge 1$. The lattice fields converge jointly in law in finite products of $H^{-a}_{\mathrm{loc}}$ at finitely many positive times and bounded continuous initial conditions. (Theorem 1.2, p. 6, and Remark 6.5, p. 36.)

3. **[Comparison with respect to initial data](../Critical2DKPZStatements/CriticalKPZComparison.lean)** — For fixed $t>0$, bounded Lipschitz initial data $h_0,h'_0$ and a smooth compactly supported test $\psi$, both the regularized pairings at each $0\lt \delta\lt t$ and their KPZ limits satisfy almost surely $|H_t^\vartheta(h_0,\psi)-H_t^\vartheta(h'_0,\psi)|\le\|\psi\|_1\|h_0-h'_0\|_\infty$; the regularized inequality uses the corresponding regularized pairings. (Comparison principle after Theorem 1.2, pp. 6–7.)

4. **[Small-scale Gaussian limit](../Critical2DKPZStatements/CriticalKPZSmallScaleCLT.lean)** — For flat initial data, fixed $t>0$, $x\in\mathbb R^2$ and nonnegative $\varphi\in C_c^\infty$, the variable $H_t^\vartheta(\varphi_\varepsilon^{(x)})/\sqrt{\log\log(1/\varepsilon)}$ converges in law as $\varepsilon\downarrow0$ to a centered Gaussian of variance $(\int\varphi)^2$, where $\varphi_\varepsilon^{(x)}(y)=\varepsilon^{-2}\varphi((y-x)/\varepsilon)$. (Theorem 1.4(i), p. 7.)

5. **[Covariance kernel](../Critical2DKPZStatements/CriticalKPZCovarianceKernel.lean)** — At each fixed $t>0$ the flat-initial field has a pointwise nonnegative radial covariance kernel $C_t^\vartheta\in L^2_{\mathrm{loc}}$, continuous away from zero, such that $\mathrm{Cov}(H_t^\vartheta(f),H_t^\vartheta(g))=\int f(x)g(y)C_t^\vartheta(x-y)\,dx\,dy$ for smooth compactly supported $f,g$ and $C_t^\vartheta(z)/\log\log(1/|z|)\to1$ as $z\to0$, $z\ne0$. (Theorem 1.4(ii), pp. 7–8.)

6. **[No signed-measure representation](../Critical2DKPZStatements/CriticalKPZNotSignedMeasure.lean)** — At every fixed positive time, the flat-initial field almost surely cannot be represented on all smooth compactly supported tests as the difference of two locally finite Borel measures on $\mathbb R^2$. (Proposition 1.5, p. 8.)

7. **[Non-Gaussianity](../Critical2DKPZStatements/CriticalKPZNonGaussian.lean)** — For every fixed $t>0$ and nonzero nonnegative smooth compactly supported $\psi$, the law of the flat-initial pairing $H_t^\vartheta(\psi)$ differs from every Gaussian law, including every constant law of variance zero. (Theorem 1.6, p. 8.)

8. **[Spatial invariance and scaling](../Critical2DKPZStatements/CriticalKPZInvariance.lean)** — The flat-initial field has almost-everywhere measurable test evaluations whose finite-dimensional laws are invariant under translations and determinant-one rotations and satisfy $(H_{a t_i}^\vartheta(S_a\varphi_i))_i\overset d=(H_{t_i}^{\vartheta+\log a}(\varphi_i))_i$ for $a>0$, where $S_a\varphi(y)=a^{-1}\varphi(y/\sqrt a)$ and the right-hand side may be any associated KPZ process of any SHF at the shifted parameter. (Proposition 1.8, pp. 8–9.)

9. **[Ehrhard-type KPZ marginals](../Critical2DKPZStatements/CriticalKPZIsEhrhardType.lean)** — For each fixed $t>0$ and nonnegative smooth compactly supported $\varphi$, including the zero test, $H_t^\vartheta(\varphi)$ is almost-everywhere measurable and has an Ehrhard-type probability law, with constant laws included. (Proposition 3.8, p. 19.)

10. **[Lattice centring](../Critical2DKPZStatements/KpzLatticeCentringAsymptotic.lean)** — The flat-initial lattice centring at endpoint zero satisfies $c_t^{\vartheta,(N)}=-\tfrac12\log\log N+O_{\vartheta,t}(1)$ as $N\to\infty$ for every fixed $\vartheta\in\mathbb R$ and $t>0$. (Equation (1.16), p. 6, and Lemma 4.3, p. 24.)

11. **[Gaussian convexity](../Critical2DKPZStatements/GaussianConvexity.lean)** — For every finite convex real function $F$ of a finite-dimensional Gaussian vector, including a singular Gaussian vector, with $\mathbb E e^{qF}\lt \infty$ for all $q>0$, the function $q\mapsto q^{-1}\log\mathbb E e^{qF}$, extended by $\mathbb EF$ at zero, is convex on all of $\mathbb R$. (Theorem 3.1, p. 16.)

12. **[Ehrhard's inequality](../Critical2DKPZStatements/EhrhardInequality.lean)** — For any finite-dimensional Gaussian law $\gamma$, convex sets $A,B$, $0\le t\le1$ and real thresholds $a,b$ satisfying $\Phi(a)\le\gamma(A)$ and $\Phi(b)\le\gamma(B)$, one has $\Phi((1-t)a+tb)\le\gamma((1-t)A+tB)$, including singular Gaussian laws and the outer-measure reading for nonmeasurable convex sets. (FMP25, Theorem 2.1.)

13. **[Ehrhard-type characterization and moment bounds](../Critical2DKPZStatements/EhrhardTypeCharacterization.lean)** — Finite convex Gaussian images have Ehrhard-type laws. These are exactly the laws of $h(G)$, where $G$ is standard Gaussian and $h$ is a finite nondecreasing convex real function; this class is closed under weak convergence. Under $\mathbb E e^X=1$ and integrability of $e^X$, an Ehrhard-type $X$ is square-integrable with $\mathrm{Var}\,X\le-2\,\mathbb EX$ and $\log\mathbb E e^{-pX}\le-p\,\mathbb EX+\tfrac12p^2\,\mathrm{Var}\,X$ for $p\ge 0$. The final two bounds, $-\mathbb EX\le\tfrac12\log\mathbb E e^{2X}$ and $\mathbb E e^{-pX}\le(\mathbb E e^{2X})^{p(p+1)/2}$, additionally assume $\mathbb E e^{2X}\lt \infty$. (Lemma 3.6 and Remark 3.7, p. 18.)

14. **[Planar random-walk estimates](../Critical2DKPZStatements/SrwLocalCLTGaussianBound.lean)** — For the planar simple random walk and $n\ge1$, the additive local limit estimate $q_n(z)=2g_{n/2}(z)+O(n^{-2})$ holds uniformly on the parity class of $n$, while $q_n(z)\le Cn^{-1}e^{-c|z|^2/n}$ holds for all sites with fixed finite $C$ and $c>0$. (Lawler and Limic, *Random Walk: A Modern Introduction*, Theorem 2.3.5 (2.23) and Propositions 2.1.2 and 2.4.6.)

15. **[Critical-window second moments](../Critical2DKPZStatements/SecondMomentCriticalWindow.lean)** — For fixed $\vartheta$ and $t>0$, the flat point-to-plane partition function satisfies $\mathbb E Z_{[Nt]}(0)^2/\log N\to C_{\vartheta,t}>0$, and eventually $\sigma_N^2\mathbb E Z_k(0)^2\le C_\vartheta/\log(1+N/k)$ simultaneously for $1\le k\le N$. (Caravenna, Sun and Zygouras, *The Dickman subordinator, renewal theorems, and disordered systems*, Proposition A.2; the paper, §4.1.)

16. **[Geometric-block moments](../Critical2DKPZStatements/BlockMoments.lean)** — Let $K=\lfloor\log_\rho N\rfloor+1$, $N_0=0$, $N_i=[N\rho^{i-K}]$ for $1\le i\lt K$, $N_K=N$, and let $\widehat Z_i=\sum_y q_{N_{i-1}}(y)\,Z_{N_{i-1},N_i}(y)$ be the point-to-plane partition function on $[N_{i-1},N_i]$ averaged over the position at time $N_{i-1}$ of a walk started at $0$. For the normalized geometric-block partition functions $\widehat Z_i$ and $1\le i\le K$, including the first block of bounded length, $\mathrm{Var}(\widehat Z_i)\le C/(K+1-i)$ and $\mathbb E|\widehat Z_i-1|^4\le C/(K+1-i)^2$, with $C$ uniform for $\rho$ in a fixed compact subset of $(1,\infty)$ and $\vartheta$ in a fixed compact interval, although the eventual threshold in $N$ may depend on $\rho$. (Lemma B.2, p. 80, and Remark 7.5, p. 40.)

17. **[SHF transformations and regularity](../Critical2DKPZStatements/ShfSymmetries.lean)** — Every critical SHF is again a critical SHF (with the same parameter $\vartheta$) under simultaneous spatial translations of both endpoints, determinant-one rotations and nonnegative time shifts. Under the diffusive rescaling $Z'_{s,t}=a^{-1}(D_{a^{-1/2}}\times D_{a^{-1/2}})_*Z_{as,at}$ for $a>0$, it is a critical SHF with parameter $\vartheta+\log a$, where $D_c(y)=cy$ and $(\cdot)_*$ is the image measure. For every finite family $0\le s_i\lt t_i\le T$ it has the finite-dimensional time-reversal law for compact continuous endpoint-pair tests obtained by replacing $[s_i,t_i]$ by $[T-t_i,T-s_i]$ and exchanging endpoints, and each compact continuous test pairing is continuous in probability as $(s,t)$ varies within $0\le s\lt t$. For each fixed $0\le s\lt t$, $\delta>0$, $x\in\mathbb R^2$ and bounded continuous $h_0$, the pairing $Z_{s,t}(g_\delta^{(x)},e^{h_0})$ is positive almost surely, its logarithm is almost-everywhere measurable, and that logarithm has an Ehrhard-type law. (SHF transformations, §1 and §5; continuity in probability, §6.2.)

18. **[Delayed heat-start log-normality](../Critical2DKPZStatements/ShfLogNormality.lean)** — For each given critical SHF at fixed $\vartheta$ and each $x$, writing $F_\delta(x)=\log Z_{\delta,1}(g_\delta^{(x)},1)$, the centered variable $(F_{\varepsilon^2}(x)-\mathbb EF_{\varepsilon^2}(x))/\sqrt{\log\log(1/\varepsilon)}$ converges in law to $N(0,1)$ as $\varepsilon\downarrow0$ through $(0,1)$. (Theorem 8.1, p. 54.)

19. **[One-point moment comparison](../Critical2DKPZStatements/PropVD.lean)** — Uniformly in the starting site and $\vartheta$ on compact intervals, the smoothed partition functions at even clocks satisfy $0\le\Delta(\log\widehat Z_{s,n})\le\log(\mathbb E[\widehat Z_{s,n}^{\,2}]/\mathbb E\widehat Z_{s,n}^{-1})\le C$ on fixed macroscopic time windows, and the same logarithmic ratio is at most $C|\log\delta|^{-1/4}+o_N(1)$ for $n=[\sqrt\delta N]$ and even $s\le n$, where $\Delta(F)=-\mathbb EF-\tfrac12\mathrm{Var}F$ and $\widehat Z_{s,n}(x)=\sum_y q_s(y-x)Z_{s,n}(y)$ is the point-to-plane partition function after time $s$ with the walk's position at time $s$ averaged. (Proposition 4.1, p. 22.)

20. **[Future-profile comparison and logarithmic moments](../Critical2DKPZStatements/PropPFLogTails.lean)** — For sufficiently small $\delta>0$, with $m=[\delta N]$, $n=[\sqrt\delta N]$ and $\ell=|\log\delta|$, there are good events $G_x$ measurable with respect to the disorder through time $n$, with $P(G_x^c)=O(\ell^{-1})$; integrating over $G_x$, the unweighted future-mass $L^1$ difference is $O(\ell^{-1})$, both future-mass second moments are $O(\ell)$, and the weighted $L^1$ difference is $O(M\sqrt{\log\ell}/\ell)$ whenever $|f|\le M$. The $L^1$ and second-moment estimates are integrals restricted to $G_x$. Separately, for every finite $p\ge1$, every positive terminal weight satisfying $|\log f(z)|\le L$ at every site, and every $\varepsilon>0$, both future log masses have eventual $L^p$ bounds $C_p\log\ell+L+\varepsilon$, uniformly in the starting site; all constants are uniform on fixed compact $\vartheta$ intervals; for each small $\delta$ the good-event estimates hold for all large $N$ with a threshold uniform in $\vartheta$ on each such interval, whereas the threshold in the logarithmic-moment bounds may depend on $\delta,\vartheta,f,L,\varepsilon$. (Propositions 4.4–4.5, p. 25.)

21. **[Lattice smoothing for general initial data](../Critical2DKPZStatements/LemExtensionApproximation.lean)** — For fixed bounded continuous $h_0$ and smooth compactly supported $\varphi$, the $L^2$ norm of the difference between the tested reversed lattice field and its regularization has $\limsup_{N\to\infty}$ at most $C|\log\delta|^{-1/40}$ for small $\delta>0$, with $C$ and the small-$\delta$ cutoff uniform on fixed compact ranges of $T-t>0$ and $\vartheta$; the constant may depend on $h_0$ itself, not only on its supremum norm. (Lemma 5.3, p. 30.)

22. **[Joint finite-dimensional convergence](../Critical2DKPZStatements/ThmToyJoint.lean)** — At each fixed terminal time $T>0$ there are SHF-measurable limits $X(t,h_0,\varphi)$ of the reversed regularized pairings. For each fixed $h_0$, $\varphi$ and $a\in (0,T]$, the constants $C$ and the sufficiently small $\delta$ cutoff may depend on $\vartheta,T,h_0,\varphi,a$, but are uniform for $0\le t\le T-a$. The limits have $L^2$ error at most $C|\log(\delta/(T-t))|^{-1/40}$ uniformly for $0\le t\le T-a$ with $0\lt a\le T$, convergence in every finite $L^p$, $p\ge1$, joint lattice convergence in law for finitely many triples $(t,h_0,\varphi)$ with $0\le t\lt T$, and joint laws equal to those of $(H_{T-t_i}^\vartheta(h_{0,i},\varphi_i))_i$ for every KPZ process $H$ associated with the SHF. (Theorem 5.1, p. 28, and Remark 6.5, p. 36.)
